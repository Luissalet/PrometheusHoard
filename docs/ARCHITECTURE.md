# Architecture

```
browser / MCP bridge / Faustus
        │  HTTP (127.0.0.1:5205)
        ▼
  main.py ─ api/ui.py (overview, endpoints, files bytes, /api/ui/call) ─ api/agent.py (Bearer, MCP)
        │
  agent_tools.py ── one catalogue of tools
        │
  services.py ── Settings · Cluster · Files · Models · Jobs · Recipes · Power · Serving · Clients
        │
  transport.py ── one paramiko SSH connection per Spark (commands + SFTP), ~/.ssh/config with Include, optional jump host
        │
  remote/*.py ── helpers run on the Spark with python3: probe, models, jobs, fsops
```

- **cluster.py**: nodes from the settings, a poller (every `poll_s` while someone looks, `idle_poll_s` otherwise), rates computed between two probes (CPU from `/proc/stat`, network from `/proc/net/dev`), a short history per Spark, and `fabric_peer_ip(a, b)`: the address of `b` on a CX7 subnet that `a` also has.
- **files.py**: SFTP explorer, home-only writes, trash with metadata (`<trash>/<id>/.prometheus-trash.json`).
- **jobs.py**: detached jobs on a Spark (`<remote_dir>/jobs/<id>/{cmd,pid,log,rc,watch}`); progress = size of the watched folder / expected bytes.
- **models.py**: inventory (folders with weights in the models folders and the Hugging Face cache), `hf download` jobs, `rsync` copies over CX7.
- **recipes.py**: recipe folders → deployments. Start: conflicts check (Sparks used by another running recipe, free memory), upload, `start.sh` per Spark in order as jobs, wait for the head's health URL. State in `data/state.json`; a recipe that answers without having been started here is shown as running (external).
- **power.py**: lock (`loginctl`), sleep/shutdown/restart (`sudo -n systemctl …`, polkit fallback), wake-on-LAN from the remembered MAC (`data/macs.json`).
- **serving.py**: live inference figures. A parser of the Prometheus text page (only `vllm:` lines, quoted labels with commas and escapes, `+Inf`, NaN; `*_created` ignored) and the `Serving` sampler, owned by `Services`. A thread refreshes the endpoint list of `Recipes.endpoints()` at most every 15 s and scrapes `<server>/metrics` (the `/v1` of the base URL dropped; 2 s timeout, an injectable text getter, `world.http_text` in the demo) every 2 s while the page or the `serving_stats` tool asked in the last 60 s (`touch`) and every 10 s otherwise; without the thread (`background=False`, the tests) the view samples on demand. Per endpoint it keeps 15 minutes of samples (counters, gauges and histogram buckets summed across label sets) and derives, over the last ~10 s, decode tokens/s (Δgeneration), prefill tokens/s (Δ(prompt − cached)), running, waiting, KV %, tokens per step (`1 + Δaccepted/Δdrafts`) and acceptance (`Δaccepted/Δdraft tokens`), both falling back to the whole life of the server and flagged `spec_cumulative`; over the last 5 min, p50/p95 of each latency histogram with Prometheus' linear interpolation (whole life when nothing moved); preemptions in the window and in 5 min; and the sparkline series (last 10 min, at most 120 points). An endpoint whose `/metrics` fails is reported with `ok=false` and the error and does not affect the others; its figures go blank after 10 s without a good scrape. `GET /api/serving` and the `serving_stats` tool return `{endpoints, totals, poll_s, now}`; `sparks_overview` reads only the sampler's cache.
- **clients.py**: who uses each model server. vLLM never records who asks but its uvicorn logs every request (`INFO:     <ip>:<port> - "POST /v1/chat/completions HTTP/1.1" 200 OK`), so `Clients` follows the endpoints `Serving` already follows (`Serving.infos()`, the same cached list) and a collector thread reads, per endpoint and never on a request, the lines written since a cursor with `remote/accesslog.py` on the head Spark: `docker logs --timestamps --since <cursor>` of the first container among the candidates `Recipes.log_containers` gives (the id in the health script's `memory_guard.container_id`, the recipe's `container`, the head's containers that publish the port or run `--port N`, the only engine container), `sudo -n docker` when the plain command is denied (never a password; the answer says which was used and the next read starts with it), only access lines newer than the cursor, at most 20000, plus `$SSH_CONNECTION`'s client address (the address of this PC as the Sparks see it; dropped behind a jump host). Every ~20 s while the Serving page or tools were used in the last 2 min, every 2 min otherwise; a read that fails (no container, docker denied, SSH down) is reported on the card and retried after a pause. The parser (`parse_line`) takes lines with or without docker's RFC 3339 stamp, bracketed or bare IPv6, IPv4-mapped addresses, and ignores anything else; `classify` gives the API kind (chat, completions, responses, messages, embeddings, other) or *poll* (`GET /health`, `/v1/models`, `/metrics`...). Per address it keeps minute buckets of counts per kind plus errors (non-2xx) for 24 h (the rolling hour sums the buckets that end inside it), first and last seen; loopback addresses are only counted (*local probes*). The cursor is the stamp of the newest line in nanoseconds (the lines stamped exactly at it are remembered, so an overlapping read never counts twice; a quiet log moves it to five seconds before the Spark's clock) and the Spark's clock skew is measured on every read. Names: the `client_names` setting `{address: name}` (`client_name_set` tool, edited in place on the card) wins; otherwise an address of this PC (the SSH client address, the address the system routes to the server, the local address of the connections below) is «Este PC» and an address of a Spark (from its probed interfaces and `api_host`) takes the Spark's name. This PC in detail: while someone looks (every 2 s) `local_processes` lists the ESTABLISHED TCP connections (`psutil.net_connections`) whose remote end is a server's resolved host and port and maps their pid to a program (`process_label`: `-m x_hoard` → «X's Hoard», Faustus and the assistants by their command line or folder, `node`/`python` by their script, the executable name otherwise); `AccessDenied` on the table or on a process is shown as such, never raised. `clients` travels in `GET /api/serving` and `serving_stats`; `data/clients.json` holds the 24 h buckets and cursors (saved at most every 60 s). Limits: one address is one device (programs on other computers are not told apart, a proxy hides its clients), the log only has what the container wrote since it started, and the local list is a snapshot of what is connected now.
- **speedcard.py**: the speed card and the capacity card of a model server. `benchmark` (asyncio + httpx, an injectable transport) sends streaming `chat/completions` with `stream_options.include_usage` at each concurrency level (1, 8, 16, 64 by default): unique prompts (a random nonce first), `max_tokens` fixed (256) and `ignore_eos` (dropped for the rest of the run when the first warm-up round is answered 400/422), one discarded warm-up round and `rounds` (3) measured ones per level. A request is the time to its first non-empty delta of any kind (content or reasoning) and its tokens (`usage.completion_tokens`, else the number of deltas: `tokens_source`); a round is its wall time, aggregate tokens per second (successful requests only), the median of per-stream `(tokens-1)/decode time` and TTFT p50/p95; a level is the median over its rounds (a round where everything failed is out of the medians and in the error count). `SpeedCards` runs it with `Jobs.start_local` (below), refuses a second run on the same endpoint (`busy`), saves finished cards (not cancelled ones) in `data/speedcards.json` (`{cards: {<base url>: [last 20]}}`) and builds the capacity card (`capacity_card`: `max_model_len` from `/v1/models`, `vllm:cache_config_info` → `num_gpu_blocks` × `block_size`, the recipe's `context_verified` / `measured`; cached 10 s; a source that fails leaves its fields empty and lists the error). Tools `speed_card` (read) and `speed_card_run` (`confirm`).
- **jobs.py, local jobs**: `Jobs.start_local(node, work, kind, title)` runs `work(LocalJob)` on a thread of this app and tracks it like any job (same record, `jobs_list`, `job_get`, `job_cancel`, events); `LocalJob.report(progress, line)` feeds the progress bar and the log, `cancelled` is set by `cancel`. They are never refreshed from a Spark and a restart of the app marks the ones still running as `lost`.
- **xidwatch.py**: the GPU error watcher. A thread wakes every second and, when `xid_watch` is on and `xid_interval_s` (60) has passed, runs `journalctl -k --since <cursor> --no-pager -o short-iso` on each enabled Spark (first scan `2 minutes ago`; the same command prints the Spark's `date`, which minus 5 s becomes the next cursor; a Spark that is off, unreachable or whose journal is unreadable is reported in `nodes` and keeps its cursor). `classify` keeps lines with `NVRM: Xid (PCI:...): <code>`, `NV_ERR_GPU_IN_FULLCHIP_RESET` / `FULLCHIP_RESET`, or `GSP` with error / timeout / fail; events `{id, node, t, stamp, line, kind, xid, pci, meaning, notified}` are deduplicated by (Spark, line) and bounded (500) in `data/xid_events.json` together with the cursors. New events of a Spark make one notification through `hoard_link.fam_notify` (hub facet `notify`, priority high, group `gpu-xid`; not in demo mode; `notified` says whether the hub took it). Nothing here changes a Spark. Read with the `xid_events` tool; `sparks_overview` carries `xid` (count in 24 h per Spark).
- **fake.py**: the invented cluster for the demo and the tests (also answers streaming completions, `vllm:cache_config_info` and `journalctl -k` from `world.kernel_log`).

Data folder (`data/`): `settings.json`, `state.json`, `jobs.json`, `macs.json`, `secrets.json` (Hugging Face token), `serving.json`, `clients.json`, `speedcards.json`, `xid_events.json`, `mcp-token`, `logs/`.

`serving.json` holds the cumulative tokens served per endpoint (prompt, generated, cached, requests), saved at most every 30 s and on exit with `write_json_atomic`: `offset` + `last` seen per counter, so a counter that drops (the container restarted) adds the last value seen to the offset and the totals keep growing; plus the `since` stamp and an overall total over every endpoint ever seen. Limits: counters live as long as the server's container, the totals only count what Prometheus' page showed while this app ran, and the in-memory samples (hence the charts) are lost on restart.

Recipe measurements pass unchanged through `recipes_list` (UI and MCP). `client/src/measurements.js` formats units and nested fields in Spanish and English; `RecipeMeasurements.jsx` presents a short summary and expandable full observations. This is presentation of recorded evidence, not a runtime context or accuracy certification.
